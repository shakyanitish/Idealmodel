ABOUT OUR IMS

Elevate your knowledge, cultivate your expertise, and embrace growth.

Established in 2048 B.S. (1991 A.D.), Ideal Model School was founded by experienced and dedicated leaders. Our mission is to create an environment fostering systematic, physical, mental, social, and overall personality development.

We cultivate each child's unique talents and skills, ensuring a harmonious blend of learned lessons and lived experiences. Our goal is to nurture responsible citizens, guided by spiritual and ethical values.

Beyond the classroom - unleash your creativity

At Ideal Model School, education goes beyond the classroom. Our diverse extracurricular activities offer students opportunities to explore sports, arts, leadership, and community service. From promoting a healthy lifestyle through sports to fostering creativity in arts, our programs aim to nurture well-rounded individuals. Leadership initiatives impart valuable life skills, and community service projects instill empathy and social responsibility.

Join us on a journey beyond textbooks, where students discover passions, develop leadership qualities, and build lasting friendships. Our extracurricular offerings complement classroom learning, providing a holistic education for success in every facet of life.
